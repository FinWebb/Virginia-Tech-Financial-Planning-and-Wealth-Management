02 — The Major

Built to put you in front of the CFP® exam — and a client.

The Financial Planning and Wealth Management (CFPF) major is designed for students who want to pursue a career as a financial planning professional, working with individuals, families, foundations, and trusts. As a CFP Board–Registered curriculum, the major combines traditional finance coursework with the exact educational requirements needed to sit for the Certified Financial Planner® examination after graduation — no extra coursework required.

124Total Credit Hours
37Major-Specific Credits
B.S.Bachelor of Science in Business, Finance
CFP®Board–Registered Curriculum

What you’ll study

Beyond the core finance sequence, the major layers on the technical and interpersonal skills financial planners use every day:

  • Financial Planning for Professionals
  • Risk & Insurance
  • Wills, Trusts, and Estates
  • Retirement Planning
  • Tax for Financial Planners
  • Financial Planning Technology & Modeling
  • Client Relationship Management
  • Financial Planning Applications
  • International Financial Management

Get involved

Financial planning is a relationship business, and Pamplin students build those muscles years before graduation — most visibly through the Financial Planning Society:

  • Financial Planning Society (FPS)

Mission & Core Values

The Financial Planning Society at Virginia Tech

The FPS exists to foster professional development, mentorship, and community building for financial planning students — bridging the gap between classroom learning and real-world practice.

01

Professional Development

Gaining real world experience by interacting and hearing from firms weekly, student-led professional development workshops, the Etiquette Dinner, and more.

02

Mentorship

Peer mentor groups, alumni matching, one-on-one support networks — connecting current students with the Hokies already in the field.

03

Community Building

Social events, sporting events, and the lasting friendships that come from building a career alongside your classmates.

Why Join FPS

Come for the career prep, stay for the community.

FPS is where Virginia Tech students go beyond the classroom. Every Monday, the chapter brings in professional speakers, Q&A sessions, networking opportunities, internships, career insights, and community outreach — all in one club. You get hands-on access to firms, a built-in mentor and peer network from day one, and a genuine community.

Every Monday

  • Professional speakers from firms across the industry
  • Live Q&A sessions with practitioners
  • Networking opportunities and internship leads
  • Career insights and community outreach

Throughout the year

  • The Etiquette Dinner
  • Case study competitions
  • Trips to conferences around the country
  • Socials
  • Scholarships for financial planning & wealth management students

The opportunities here for anyone pursuing financial planning and wealth management are endless — and we’re not a selective org. That’s exactly what makes us great: anyone who wants to grow professionally and make real connections belongs here. FPS gives you the experience, the alumni network, and the friendships that last well beyond graduation.
